About GEOPOLIS

An independent space for rigorous writing on politics, policy and the changing world order.

GEOPOLIS is an independent, student-led publication focused on geopolitics, international relations, security, public policy and society. We aim to make serious research and analysis accessible to students, scholars and interested readers.

Our purpose

We publish well-researched articles, analytical essays, research notes, reviews and academic work that helps readers understand contemporary political and strategic developments.

Our approach

GEOPOLIS values clarity, evidence, intellectual honesty and respectful disagreement. Contributors should distinguish verified facts from interpretation, cite credible sources and acknowledge uncertainty where it exists.

Who can contribute

We welcome work from students, researchers, educators and early-career writers with a serious interest in public affairs. Visit Submit an Article to learn how to contribute.
