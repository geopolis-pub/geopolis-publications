Editorial Board

Guiding rigorous, accessible and ethically produced public-interest writing.

The GEOPOLIS Editorial Board is responsible for maintaining the publication’s editorial standards, reviewing submissions and supporting contributors through the publication process.

Editorial responsibilities

  • Assess submissions for relevance, clarity and originality.
  • Review factual claims, sources and citations.
  • Request revisions where needed.
  • Apply the Editorial Policy and Publication Ethics standards consistently.
  • Publish corrections or clarifications when a substantive error is identified.
