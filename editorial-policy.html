Editorial Policy

GEOPOLIS is an independent, student-led publication on geopolitics, international relations and public policy. This Editorial Policy describes how content is reviewed and published.

Submission Review

All submitted articles, dissertations and analysis pieces are reviewed by the editorial team for argumentative rigour, factual accuracy and relevance before publication. Reviewers may request revisions before acceptance.

Sourcing

Contributors are expected to cite credible primary and secondary sources. Claims of fact should be verifiable; opinion and analysis should be clearly distinguished from reportage.

Corrections

If a factual error is identified after publication, GEOPOLIS will issue a visible correction on the article page as soon as possible.

Independence

GEOPOLIS does not accept payment in exchange for favourable coverage. Editorial decisions are made independently of any institutional, political or commercial affiliation.
