Write for GEOPOLIS

GEOPOLIS welcomes original articles, analytical essays, policy notes, book reviews, research summaries and dissertations on India, world affairs, geopolitics, international relations, security and society.

Before you submit

  • Submit original work that has not been published elsewhere.
  • Use a clear title and a concise abstract or excerpt.
  • Support factual claims with credible sources and citations.
  • Include your name, institutional affiliation and a short biography.
  • Upload your manuscript in PDF, DOC or DOCX format.
  • Provide a cover image only if you have the right to use it.
  • Disclose relevant financial, institutional or personal conflicts of interest.

Editorial review: Uploaded PDFs and Word documents are received by the GEOPOLIS editorial team for review. Submission does not guarantee publication. Editors may request revisions for clarity, sourcing, accuracy or house style.

Privacy and originality: By submitting, you confirm that the material is your original work, that you have permission to share any attached files or images, and that the information you provide may be used by GEOPOLIS solely to review and communicate about your submission. Please do not include sensitive personal information that is not necessary for editorial review.
