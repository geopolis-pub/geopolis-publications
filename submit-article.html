Submit an Article

Contribute original analysis, research and public-interest writing to GEOPOLIS.

What we publish

GEOPOLIS welcomes original articles, analytical essays, policy notes, book reviews, research summaries and dissertations related to India, world affairs, geopolitics, international relations, security and society.

Submission requirements

  • Submit original work that has not been published elsewhere.
  • Use a clear title and short excerpt.
  • Support factual claims with reliable sources and citations.
  • Include the author’s name, institutional affiliation and a short biography.
  • Provide an appropriate cover image only if you have permission to use it.
  • Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.

How to submit

Send your proposal or completed draft to geopolis.editor@gmail.com with the subject line “GEOPOLIS Article Submission”.

Editorial process

Submissions are assessed for relevance, originality, clarity and sourcing. Editors may suggest revisions. Publication is not guaranteed, and accepted work may be edited for clarity and house style with the contributor’s knowledge.

By submitting, you confirm that the work is original and complies with our Publication Ethics and Editorial Policy.
